NEET PG Weightage

  • Med, Surg, OBG, Paeds 90~100 Qs
  • Patho, Micro, Pharma 40~50 Qs
  • PSM, ENT, Ophtha, FMT, Ortho, Radio 30-35 Qs
  • Anatomy, Physio, Biochem 25~30 Qs
  • Derma, Anaes, Psych, Radiology 6~10 Qs

FMGE Structure

300 MCQs, Computer-based. Two 2.5-hour sessions (150 each). 1 mark per correct. Zero negative marking. Pass: 150/300 (50%).

UPSC CMS Syllabus

Paper I — General Medicine & Paediatrics (250 marks, 120 Qs)

96 Qs from General Medicine, 24 from Paediatrics. Topics: Cardiology, Respiratory, GIT, GU, Neurology, Haematology, Endocrinology, Metabolic, Infections, Nutrition, Dermatology, MSK, Psychiatry, Emergency Medicine, Common Poisoning, Tropical Medicine, Critical Care.

Paper II — Surgery, OBG & PSM (250 marks, 120 Qs)

40 Qs each from Surgery (incl. ENT, Ophtha, Ortho), OBG (Obstetrics, Gynaecology, Family Planning), and PSM (Epidemiology, Biostatistics, National Programmes, Environmental Health, Occupational Health).

Negative Marking:

1/3 deducted for wrong answers. No penalty for unattempted. Paper I: 120 Qs max 250 marks. Personality Test: 100 marks.
